MeowTabby certification testing notes

Purpose
MeowTabby translates web pages, selected text, PDF documents and subtitles. Users can compare original and translated text.

Testing
1. Open the extension popup and settings. Choose a translation provider and target language.
2. For a provider requiring an API key, enter your own valid key. Built-in hosted AI requires signing into a MeowTabby account with sufficient credit.
3. Open an ordinary English article and use the floating translation button. Compare the original and translated paragraphs.
4. Select an English sentence to open selection translation.
5. Open the PDF translation page from the extension, choose a text-based PDF, and translate a short page range. Compare original and translated pages, including formulas and multi-column paragraphs.
6. The layout model is downloaded on first use from Hugging Face or the configured mirror, then cached. This download requires network access and is about 75 MB.
7. Visit https://netxmeow.top/meowtabby/support/ to test feedback. The support form may require login and Cloudflare verification.
8. Hosted AI requests are processed by our backend and forwarded to the model provider. Hosted AI credit is separate from installing the extension.
9. The browser side panel currently shows a coming-soon page; it is not advertised as a translation feature.
10. Google Drive synchronization is removed. Local configuration import/export and local backups remain available.
